Privacy Policy

The short version

  • We collect your phone number, your name, the area you work or hire in, and your job activity — because the marketplace cannot match anyone without them.
  • We use your location only while the app is open, and only to show work near you. You can refuse, and the app still works.
  • Workers may give a National ID (NIDA) number for verification. It is optional, it is never shown to other users, and we use it for nothing else.
  • We do not handle your money and we charge you nothing. Employers and workers agree a price in the app and pay each other directly, outside it, so we hold no card, bank or mobile money details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we run no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • You can delete your account and your data yourself, at any time, from inside the app — no need to contact us.

1. Who we are

MchongoFasta is a daily-work marketplace that connects verified workers with households and small businesses in Tanzania. In this policy, "we", "us" and "MchongoFasta" mean MchongoFasta, the operator of the app and this website, and the data controller responsible for the personal data described here.

This policy is written to meet Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and the Google Play User Data policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Phone number. You sign in with a Tanzanian phone number and a one-time code (OTP) sent to it. This is how we identify your account.
  • Name. Your full name if you are a worker, or your business name if you are an employer.
  • Role. Whether you use MchongoFasta to find work or to hire.
  • Area. The part of Dar es Salaam you work or hire in.
  • Work categories. The kinds of work you offer, such as cleaning, delivery, care or repairs.
  • National ID (NIDA) number. Optional. See section 4.

2.2 Information created by using the app

  • Job activity. Tasks you post, jobs you apply for, matches, and the status of that work.
  • Ratings and reviews. Feedback exchanged after a job, and the trust score calculated from it.

2.3 Location

If you grant permission, the app reads your approximate or precise device location to show jobs and workers near you and to place them on a map. We read it only while the app is open and in use. We never read your location in the background. You can refuse or later withdraw the permission in your device settings; the app continues to work, and you can browse by area instead.

2.4 Technical information

When the app or website contacts our servers we automatically receive an IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, and the date and time of the request. We use this to keep the service running, to diagnose faults and to detect abuse.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • No payment card, bank account or mobile money credentials.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or SMS content.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. National ID and verification

A National Identification Number (NIDA) is a sensitive identifier, so we treat it separately from the rest of your profile:

  • It is optional. You can use MchongoFasta without giving it. Verified workers are shown a verification badge, which tends to attract more hires.
  • It is used only to confirm you are who you say you are. Nothing else.
  • It is never shown to other users. Employers see a verification badge, not your ID number.
  • It is never sold, rented or shared for marketing. We disclose it only where the law requires it.
  • You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us; we then delete the number and remove the verification badge.

5. Who we share it with

5.1 Other users

The marketplace only works if people can see who they might work with. Other users see your name or business name, your role, your area, your work categories, your ratings, and whether you are verified. They do not see your phone number until a match is made, and they never see your NIDA number or your exact location.

5.2 Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our instructions:

  • Google Maps Platform — renders the map and place data. Google receives the map requests your device makes, including its IP address and the map area being viewed. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the app's typefaces. Google receives your IP address when a font is fetched.
  • Vercel Inc. — hosts our API and this website.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores our database.

5.3 Legal and safety

We disclose personal data where we are required to by Tanzanian law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud or protect someone's safety.

5.4 Business transfers

If MchongoFasta is merged, acquired or restructured, personal data may transfer to the new owner. We will tell you before that happens and before this policy changes.

5.5 What we never do

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

6. Payments

MchongoFasta does not process payments and does not charge you anything. The employer and the worker agree a price in the app and then settle it directly between themselves, outside MchongoFasta, by whatever means they choose. We are not a party to that payment and we take no fee or commission from it.

Because of this we never collect, hold or transmit card numbers, bank account details or mobile money credentials, and we hold no financial records about you at all. The app is free to use for both workers and employers.

If that ever changes — if we introduce in-app payments or start charging a fee — we will update this policy, name any payment provider involved, and tell you before the change takes effect.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as your account is open.
  • Job and rating history — kept while your account is open, because trust scores depend on it.
  • NIDA number — kept only while your account is verified, and deleted when you withdraw it or close your account.
  • Technical logs — up to 12 months.
  • After you delete your account — removed immediately, except anything we must keep by law, which we isolate and keep no longer than the law requires.

8. How we protect it

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our database is access-controlled and reachable only by the application and by staff who need it. We keep the number of people with access small.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 you have the right to:

  • be told what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted — you can do this yourself in the app (see section 10);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, a particular use;
  • withdraw consent you have given — for example your NIDA number — without affecting what we did lawfully before you withdrew it;
  • receive your data in a portable form; and
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

Write to {{ contactEmail }} to use any of these. We answer within 30 days and we do not charge for it. We may ask you to confirm control of your registered phone number before we act, so that nobody can use these rights against you.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your MchongoFasta account yourself, at any time, without contacting us:

  1. Open the MchongoFasta app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

What is deleted: your profile, phone number, name, area, work categories, NIDA number, job applications and postings, and your ratings.

What may be kept: records we are required by law to retain, and aggregated statistics that can no longer identify you. Anything kept is isolated from the live service and deleted once the legal reason ends.

11. Where your data is stored

Our hosting and database providers operate data centres outside Tanzania, so your personal data is transferred and stored abroad. We only use providers that commit contractually to protect it to a standard consistent with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, and we transfer no more than the service needs.

12. Age limit

MchongoFasta is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, whether as a worker or as an employer. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app changes, and this page always shows the current version. If a change materially affects your rights — for example if we begin processing payments or add a new category of data — we will notify you in the app or by SMS before it takes effect.
