The idea

Daily work should be trusted, local, and instant.

Too many short-term jobs in Dar still run on WhatsApp forwards and unverified strangers. MchongoFasta builds a marketplace where workers prove identity and skill, employers post clearly, and matching happens on a map — then payment stays in-app.

01

Browse on a map

See open mchongo around you first, then switch to a clean list when you prefer.

02

Two clear sides

Guests find jobs by default. Employers sign in separately to hire. Applying requires login.

03

Trust before work

National ID, address checks, ratings, and references protect both sides of every task.

04

Pay with confidence

Commissions, premium visibility, and in-app ads fund the platform while workers get paid fast.

For workers

Find mchongo. Prove trust. Get paid.

  • Discover nearby jobs on map or list
  • Sign in only when you are ready to apply
  • Complete verification for better matches
  • Track earnings and withdraw securely

Worker journey

  1. Browse open tasks as a guest
  2. Tap a job → sign in to apply
  3. Finish verification for priority matching
  4. Complete work and receive payout

For employers

Hire reliable help in minutes.

  • Post cleaning, logistics, care, or technical tasks
  • Review verified applicants with ratings
  • Track progress and release payment
  • Build a trusted shortlist for next time

Employer journey

  1. Open the employer invite from the app
  2. Sign in and set up your hiring profile
  3. Post a task with budget and timing
  4. Match, confirm, and pay in one flow
